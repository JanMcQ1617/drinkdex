Mai Tai

Nº 0145

Tiki & tropical cocktail from Oakland, USA

The spec

Shaken · 5 ingredients
  • 2 oz60 mlAged rum
  • 1 oz30 mlLime juice (fresh)
  • 0.5 oz15 mlOrange curaçao
  • 0.5 oz15 mlOrgeat syrup
  • 1 cupCrushed ice · plus more to serve
  1. Squeeze the lime and keep one spent half-shell for the garnish.
  2. Add the rum, lime juice, curaçao, and orgeat to a shaker with about 1 cup of crushed ice.
  3. Shake hard for 8-10 seconds.
  4. Pour everything, ice and all, into a double rocks glass and top with more crushed ice.
  5. Nestle the spent lime shell on top like a little island, tuck a mint sprig beside it, and serve with a straw.
Garnish
Spent lime shell and a fresh mint sprig
