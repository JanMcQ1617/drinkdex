## Who can use Sipply

You must be **18 or older**, and old enough to drink legally where you live —
whichever is higher. Sipply is rated 18+ and is about alcoholic drinks
throughout.

One account per person. Do not share your account, and do not let anyone else
use it. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

## The one rule that matters most

**There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behaviour on
Sipply.**

That is not a formality. Content that breaks it is removed and the account
behind it is suspended or permanently deleted.

Specifically, do not post or send:

- Sexual content, nudity, or anything sexualising a minor
- Harassment, threats, bullying, or targeted abuse
- Hate speech, or attacks based on race, ethnicity, religion, disability,
  sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, or national origin
- Violent or graphic content
- Anything encouraging or glamorising **underage drinking**, drink-driving,

- Spam, scams, or attempts to sell anything — including alcohol
- Someone else's photos or personal information posted without their consent
- Impersonation of another person or account

Do not try to break the app either: no scraping, no automated accounts, no
attempts to reach data that is not yours, and no reverse-engineering of the
app to get around the rules above.

## Reporting and blocking

Every post that is not yours has a **"..."** menu with **Report** and
**Block**.

**Report** asks for a reason and sends it to us. Reports are reviewed and
acted on **within 24 hours**. Content that breaks these terms is removed, and
the account is suspended or deleted depending on what it did.

**Block** takes effect immediately. The person you block cannot see you and
you cannot see them, any follow between you is dropped in both directions,
and they are not told. You do not need our involvement to block someone.

If something is urgent, email **mcqueeny1617@gmail.com** rather than waiting.

**You own what you post.** Your photos and captions stay yours.

By posting, you give Sipply permission to store your content and show it to
other people signed in to Sipply — which is what makes the app work. That
permission is limited to running the app. We do not sell your content,
license it to anyone else, or use it to advertise.

That permission ends when you delete the content or your account. Copies may
survive briefly in backups before they age out.

**Treat anything you post as visible to everyone signed in to Sipply.**

## Drinking

Sipply is a reference and a diary. It is not advice.

Drink responsibly, know your limits, and never drive after drinking. If you
think you have a problem with alcohol, please talk to a doctor or a support
service in your country — Sipply is not equipped to help with that and does
not try to be.

**Nothing in the app is medical, nutritional, or legal advice.**

## The drink information itself

Recipes, ingredients, ABV figures, origins, and classifications are compiled
from public reference sources and are provided for interest. They are not
guaranteed to be accurate, current, or complete, and they vary by producer,
vintage, batch, and country.

Do not rely on Sipply for anything where being wrong matters — allergens and
alcohol content especially. Check the label.

immediate and cannot be undone. You do not need to ask us.

We may suspend or delete an account that breaks these terms. Where it is
reasonable to do so we will say why, but conduct in the list above may be
actioned without warning.

We may also stop running Sipply. If that happens we will give notice in the
app and a way to get your content out first.
