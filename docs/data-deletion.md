# Deleting your data

**Last updated: 1 October 2026**

How to delete everything Sipply holds about you, what that removes, and how
to remove Sipply from Facebook, Google or Apple if you signed in with one of
them.

---

## Delete your Sipply account

In the app, open **Profile → Settings** (the gear, top right) **→ Delete
account**, and confirm.

It happens straight away, it cannot be undone, and you do not need to email
anyone first. If a photo cannot be removed, because the connection drops,
say, the account stays and the app tells you, so you can try again once
you have a signal.

## What that removes

From our servers:

- your profile and profile photo, every post and every photo you uploaded,
  your likes, your saved posts, your follows in both directions, and your
  blocks
- the drinks you added and the photos you sent with them
- your invite links, the hashes that let people find you by phone number
  or Instagram username, and the record kept for the matching limits
- your sign-in details: the phone number you signed in with, if you did,
  and everything Google or Facebook sent when you connected it: your name,
  your email address, the link to your profile picture, and the number
  Google or Facebook issues for you

From the phone you delete from:

- the list of your Facebook friends who are on Sipply, and Facebook's
  access key if it is still in memory
- your collection, the drinks you added, the photos kept for them, and
  what Sipply remembered there for finding friends

Your Facebook friends list was never on our servers to begin with. It is
matched each time you connect or check it and never kept, and the
[Privacy Policy](privacy) explains how.

A few things outlast the account. A report someone filed about you, or
about a post of yours, keeps its own copy of what it was about, your
username included, so that it can still be reviewed. A report you filed is
kept without your identity. A drink you suggested that was already in a
monthly email stays in that email, and one already added to the Dex stays
in the Dex, without your name.

## Remove Sipply from Facebook

Deleting your Sipply account changes nothing on Facebook. To stop Facebook
sharing anything more with Sipply:

1. In the Facebook app, open the menu, then **Settings & privacy →
   Settings → Apps and websites**. On a computer, go to
   [facebook.com/settings?tab=applications](https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=applications).
2. Choose **Sipply**, then **Remove**.

From then on Facebook sends Sipply nothing more about you, and you drop
out of your Facebook friends' lists in Sipply the next time they check
Facebook. Facebook may also offer to delete anything Sipply posted for
you; Sipply never posts, so there is nothing there.

If your Sipply account was made with Continue with Facebook, delete it
first. Signing in to delete it afterwards would mean connecting Facebook
again.

If you remove Sipply from Facebook but keep your Sipply account, what
Sipply already has stays with the account until you delete it: the account
itself, and the sign-in details listed above.

## If you used Sign in with Apple

Deleting your account removes what Apple sent when you signed in. Sipply
stays listed under your Apple Account until you remove it there: on your
iPhone, open **Settings**, tap your name, then **Sign-In & Security → Sign
in with Apple**, choose Sipply, and stop using it. The menu names shift a
little between iOS versions.

## If you used Continue with Google

Deleting your account removes what Google sent when you signed in. Sipply
stays listed under your Google Account until you remove it there: go to
[myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections)
(in your Google Account, **Security → Your connections to third-party apps
& services**), choose Sipply, and delete its connection. Google renames
these pages from time to time.

## If you cannot open the app

Email **mcqueeny1617@gmail.com** and ask for your account to be deleted.
Write from the address on your Sipply account, which for an account made
with Google or Facebook is the one that Google or Facebook account uses, so
we know the request is yours. If you used Hide My Email with Sign in with
Apple, you cannot write from that address, so give your Sipply username
instead: before anything is deleted, we confirm by writing to that address,
which Apple forwards to you. The account is deleted as described above, and
you get a reply when it is done.

If your account was made with a phone number, you can sign in with that
number on any iPhone with Sipply and delete the account from Settings
there.
