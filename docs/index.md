# Sipply

**Last updated: 3 September 2026**

Sipply is a drinks field guide for iPhone. You collect entries, log what you
drink, and share it with people you follow.

This site holds the app's documents.

- **[Support](support.html)** — how to get help, what to do about a
  forgotten password, and how to delete your account.
- **[Privacy Policy](privacy.html)** — what the app stores, where it goes,
  and how to get rid of it.
- **[Terms of Use](terms.html)** — the rules for using it.

Sipply is made by one person. There is no support queue and no ticket
number — there is an email address, and it is read.
