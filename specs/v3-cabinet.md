## 0. What Jan chose, and what this spec does

Jan, 5 Oct 2026: the **Cabinet** direction, with grafts from **After hours**, to make the app "more immersive, not so bland and monotonous".

Cabinet's idea, kept whole: Sipply is a collector's cabinet made of two materials. The **lining** (wine, grained like baize) is the inside of the cabinet. A drink you have not caught is a **recess** pressed into the lining. A drink you have caught is a **mount**: bone card stock with a printed rarity rule, seated in its slot with a contact shadow. Cream paper stays the ground you read on. Collecting moves a drink from one material to the other, and that change reads at arm's length (today a collected card differs from a locked one by about 3% of its pixels).

The judges' grafts are binding, and each one has a home in this spec:

## 1. Rules

### 1.1 Jan's standing rules (non-negotiable), and how v3 keeps each

| Rule | How v3 keeps it |
|---|---|
| No ovals or pills except avatars, the camera shutter and small dots | Mounts, slots, plates, plaques, prints, panels, tags and buttons are rounded rectangles (radius 2 to 12). New circles: the 8pt tier dots and ring only (`round-ok: dot`). check-design rule 4 unchanged |
| Rounded rectangles with real 1px borders | Every mount, slot, plate, plaque, print and panel draws a 1pt edge (§3.1 lists each edge token). The mat also prints an inner rarity rule |
| No uppercase, no letterspaced capitals | No `textTransform`; the intro's literal "DISCOVER · SIP · SHARE" becomes sentence case (§9.11.6). Tracking stays only on the dex number (digits). check-design rule 8 |
| Home and Profile keep Instagram's structure, never its identity | Home: bar (+, wordmark, heart), a row of today's pours, posts (author row, photo, actions, likes, caption, time). Profile: avatar + counts + bio + actions, a highlights-slot row (here "Top shelf"), tabbed grid. No Instagram copy, marks or gradient rings |
| Sign-in sheet keeps its layout | Title bar, grouped Country/Phone box, Continue, "or", stacked outlined provider buttons, consent. Only the ground behind the sheet changes (§9.11) |
| Gilt only for legendary (and rare, at most) | Gilt is legendary only: the double rule, the plaque, the sparkle, the slot hairline. Rare is wine |
| "Not look like AI" | No glows, no glass, no blur halos, no mood gradients behind content. Gradients exist only where light physically does something: photo scrims, the photo-to-ground dissolve, the 12pt shade under a lining band, the light inside a vector face's window (§5) |

### 1.2 Engineering constraints

1. **No layout entrance animations** (`entering=` / `FadeIn*` / `ZoomIn*`). check-design rule 3 is unchanged and still allowlists only CelebrationOverlay's scrim fade.
2. **Every motion rests in the fully visible state.** Allowed: scroll-linked transforms that are identity at offset 0, press feedback, the one-shot legendary foil (rest = off the card), native sticky headers. Section 10 lists every motion in v3; there are no others.
3. **Images in small frames use `enforceEarlyResizing`**, catalogue images use `cachePolicy="disk"`, and the expo-image memory cap in `src/app/_layout.tsx` (`IMAGE_MEMORY_CACHE_BYTES`, 256 MB) stays. No screen mounts more decoded image memory than it does today (§11 is the budget, screen by screen). check-design rule 9 enforces `enforceEarlyResizing`.
4. **WCAG AA** (`scripts/check-contrast.mjs` passes and gains every pair in §4), **Dynamic Type** (§6.5), **VoiceOver labels unchanged or better** (§13).
5. `scripts/check-design.mjs` passes, extended with rules 6 to 12 (§12.1).
6. **Reels** (`EXPO_PUBLIC_REELS=off`): keep compiling and consistent; one line changes (§9.14).
7. `node_modules` is a symlink to the main checkout. **Never install anything.**

## 2. Grounds and materials

Five grounds. A screen root uses one of them (check-design rule 10).

| Ground | Token | Hex | Job | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Paper | `colors.bg` | `#F7F2EA` (unchanged) | Everything you read and write | Feed, Dex cabinet front, Profile header and grid, sheets, Settings-class screens |
| Mat | `colors.mat` | `#FBF8F2` | Bone card stock: the face of a collected drink | Mounted Dex cards, Top shelf cards, the spec card, Today's tiles, mounted thumbnails |
| Lining | `colors.lining` | `#3E0A12` (= `wineDeep`, named for its job) | The inside of the cabinet | Home head band, the interleaved module, the Dex tray, Top shelf band, sign-in backdrop, Log's collect preview, Settings member card, pinned drink action bar, trivia band |
| Cellar | `colors.liningDeep` | `#2F070D` | The recess, and the ground a lit photo settles into | Empty slots, the drink page, the Latest catch panel, the edge colour of every lit photo |
| Reel ground | `colors.reelGround` | `#0E0B0B` (unchanged) | Video and the pours viewer | Unchanged |

Media (photos and video) is not a ground: anything drawn over it uses `onMedia` (§3.2).

Rule of thumb for builders: **you read on paper, you collect in the lining, a lit drink sits in the cellar.**

### 3.2 `onMedia`: new export (the only colours allowed over a photo or video)

export const onMedia = {

| plaque neutral: `reelInk` on 0.78 over white | 9.98 | 4.5 |
| `glyphGilt` (sparkle) on 0.78 over white | 4.31 | 3.0 (glyph) |

**Never over media** (check-contrast asserts each FAILS 4.5:1 over 0.62-over-white AND that its value is not any `onMedia` text colour): `onLining` 4.41, `onLiningMuted` 2.25, `onLiningFaint` 1.63, `giltOnLining` 2.36, `rareOnLining` 2.04, `taupe` 2.95, `reelInkDim` 2.11, `wineSoft` 1.14. So rarity over a photo is always a **solid plaque**, and the only ink over media is `reelInk`.

Placement rule that makes this true: over a photo, text only ever sits on scrim alpha ≥ 0.62. The Nameplate (§7.3) enforces it structurally, with no measurement: a fixed 96pt fade (`scrimClear` → `scrimMid`) sits **above** its content, and the content box itself carries a second gradient (`scrimMid` → `scrimDeep`) as its own absolute-fill background, so however tall the name grows (Dynamic Type, three lines), every glyph is on ≥ 0.62 from the first frame. (A single gradient sized "content + 96pt" with its mid stop at 0.40 does not hold: once the content passes 144pt, a two-line name at default size, the top of the name lands above the stop.)

The drink page's title block is **not** over media: it carries an opaque `liningDeep` ground (§9.8.3), so `onLining` / `onLiningMuted` there are measured against the cellar (§4.1), never against the photo.

## 5. Materials

All material is static: Views, borders, `elevation` tokens, and react-native-svg gradients (the app has no expo-linear-gradient; SVG is how DexCard and the drink page already draw gradients).

### 5.1 Lining grain
`<Grain tone="lining" />` (§7.1.9): the existing tile at `grain.lining` opacity, mounted **under** content inside every lining surface. Never over a photo. A lining surface without grain is a bug.

### 5.2 Mount (collected)
`mat` fill; 1pt edge (`matEdge` on lining, `line` on paper); radius 8 (grid, feature), 6 (shelf), 4 (thumb); the tier's inner rule (`RARITY_META.rule`) at inset 4 / 3 / none, radius = outer − inset; on lining only, `elevation.seat`. The window inside is clipped at radius 3 (grid/feature) or 2, with a 1pt `windowEdge` hairline drawn as an overlay, on a `liningDeep` fill (a lit photo's own edge colour, so the decode moment matches).

### 5.3 Slot (not collected)
`liningDeep` fill; 1pt `slotEdge` (legendary: `slotEdgeLegendary`, so a locked legendary is visibly worth hunting); `elevation.recess`; window hairline `slotWindowEdge`.

### 5.4 Lip and shade
Where a lining band meets paper below it: a 1pt `liningLip` line along the band's bottom, and a 12pt SVG gradient `shade` → transparent below the band (`pointerEvents="none"`). Two placements: **overlay** (absolute, hanging over the next element) only where the band is a list *header*, which can be lifted with `ListHeaderComponentStyle={{ zIndex: 1 }}` (Home's head band), and **in-flow** everywhere else: the band's own last 12pt is a paper strip (with paper grain) carrying the shade. The interleaved "Not in your Dex yet" module (§9.4) is a list *item*, and an absolute child of one FlatList cell is painted over by the next cell (a zIndex inside the cell does not escape its CellRenderer), so its shade is in-flow; so is the Top shelf's on Profile (§9.9.2), with the TabStrip starting right under it.

### 5.5 Scrims
- Nameplate (bottom of a photo): two SVG linear gradients, no measurement: a 96pt fade `scrimClear` → `scrimMid` stacked directly above the content, and `scrimMid` → `scrimDeep` as the content box's own absolute-fill background (§4.4). Both `pointerEvents="none"`.
- Top scrim (status bar and back button over a photo): `scrimMid` → `scrimClear` over 120pt.
- Photo dissolve (drink page hero foot): transparent → `liningDeep` over the hero's last 200pt; plus an 80pt content-top fade (transparent → opaque `liningDeep`) that ends exactly where the title block's opaque ground begins (§9.8.3).

### 5.6 The tungsten re-light (photographs)
Baked at build time (§8), never at runtime. Catalogue photographs become a drink lit by one warm pool of tungsten (`#7C5642`) against a wall that falls off to `liningDeep` and settles to it at every edge, so a full-bleed photo has no seam with the cellar ground and a windowed one reads as a lit print. People's photos are **never** graded.

## 6. Typography

### 6.1 Families
Unchanged: Inter 400/500/600 and Playfair Display 600/700 from `assets/fonts` (latin subsets). No italic exists; `DrinkPanels`' quoted-note `fontStyle: 'italic'` is removed (it rendered upright anyway).

### 6.2 Playfair discipline
Playfair is used for **the wordmark and drink names, nothing else**, and only through the name roles in §3.6. That rules out: counts and figures (`heroFigure`, `count` are Inter tabular), section and shelf headers ("Fizz", "The spec", "Top shelf", "Not in your Dex yet" are `shelfTitle` or `sectionTitle`, Inter), sentences ("Negroni joins your Dex" is Inter with only "Negroni" in `nameInline`), avatar initials (Inter), bar titles (Inter). check-design rule 6.

### 6.3 The 11pt floor
Nothing renders below 11pt: `type.tag` (11) is the floor, plates use `dexNumber` (11), tab labels 11, avatar initials `Math.max(11, Math.round(inner * 0.4))`. Names shrink only through `DrinkName`, whose floor is 11pt; anything else that uses `minimumFontScale` chooses it so `role size × scale ≥ 11`. check-design rule 7 fails any literal `fontSize` under 11.

### 6.4 Names never truncate

A drink name never takes `numberOfLines` and never uses `adjustsFontSizeToFit`. It wraps at word boundaries and its container grows (rows stretch, panels have a min height, rails take the tallest tile). The other way a name breaks is **inside a word**: iOS character-wraps a word wider than its line, and the catalogue has such words. Measured from the font files (Playfair Display Bold), "Holunderbeergeist" is 453pt at `drinkHero` 52 (the column is 361 on a 393pt phone), 148pt at `cardName` 17 (the card's text column is ≈128 to 141), and "Feuerzangenbowle" is 224pt at `shelfName` 26 (the Latest catch column is ≈153).

So every drink name renders through **`DrinkName`** (§7.2), which takes the column width (`measure`, known from the window width at every site) and shrinks the role, never below 11pt, until the widest unbreakable run fits, using the conservative width estimate in `src/lib/textFit.ts` (§7.2.1; it never under-estimates any catalogue name, origin or abv string). Short names are untouched; only names with a long word shrink, and only as far as they must.

| Where | Role | Measure (pt) | Cap (§6.5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Feed nameplate | `nameplate` | width − 32 | 1.3 |
| Drink page, custom drink page | `drinkHero` | width − 32 | 1.2 |
| Pours viewer, collect moment | `nameLg` | footer / card text width | 1.3 |
| Latest catch | `shelfName` | (width − 32) × 0.54 − 32 | 1.3 |
| Dex card (collected) | `cardName` | cardWidth − 38 | 1.3 |
| Dex card (locked) | Inter Medium 15/20 | cardWidth − 38 | 1.3 |
| Log rows, My Bar rows, Add-a-drink rows, the post page's "From the Dex" row | `rowName` | row text column (caller passes it, §7.1.4) | 1.4 |
| Interleave prints | `printName` | 136 | 1.3 |
| Top shelf, the Dex's "Added by you" tiles, Stats' rarest entry | `miniName` | card / row text width (Top shelf: card width − 26) | 1.3 |
| Today's tiles | `tileName` | 84 (label column) | 1.3 |

The one exception, stated rather than hidden: three catalogue words ("Holunderbeergeist", "Feuerzangenbowle", "Zwetschgenwasser", 95pt even at the 11pt floor) cannot fit the two narrowest columns (Today's tiles at 84pt, Top shelf at ≈83pt on a 375pt phone), so there they break inside the word at 11pt. No other catalogue name on any surface breaks inside a word, at any Dynamic Type size up to the caps. (A name someone types for their own drink gets the same fitting, down to the same floor.)

Usernames may still ellipsize on one line (they are not drink names). Every name is spoken whole by its element's accessibility label.

### 6.5 Dynamic Type caps (`maxFontSizeMultiplier`)
1.2 for `wordmark`, `wordmarkLg`, `drinkHero`; 1.3 for anything over media (nameplate, plates, plaques, markers), Dex card text, Top shelf, Today's labels, the tab bar, `heroFigure`, `shelfName` and `nameLg`, the Latest catch panel, the Dex shelf headers (sticky, so they must not grow to cover the tray), the drink page's label band and its pinned bar label; 1.4 for `rowName`. Body text is uncapped as today. Fixed-size frames (Dex window, tiles) never clip text: labels sit outside or above the window and their containers grow; anything pinned or sticky (the drink page's action bar, shelf headers) takes its height from its content (min height, never a fixed height).

"2,089" in any string is `formatCount(TOTAL)`, never a literal.

### 6.6 Sentence case
Every displayed drink style goes through `styleLabel()` (§7.7). Rank titles from `lib/milestones` ("Barfly in Training") are proper names and stay as they are.

#### 7.2.1 `src/lib/textFit.ts`: new (package T, pure, no React)

```ts
export type Face = 'playfair' | 'inter';
/** Conservative width in points of the widest unbreakable run of `text`: split on spaces, and after
 *  hyphens and en dashes (where iOS may break). Each character is costed by class, in em:
 *  Playfair narrow 0.49, lower 0.66, cap/digit 0.90, wide 0.97; Inter narrow 0.47, lower 0.65,
 *  cap/digit 0.77, wide 1.02. Classes: narrow = i j l t f r I ' ’ . , : ; ! | ( ) / - and space;
 *  wide = m w M W æ œ Æ Œ %; cap = other capitals, digits, & – ~; lower = everything else
 *  (accents are classed by their base letter). Measured against the font files: never below the
 *  real advance for any catalogue name, origin or abv string, and at most ~25% above it on long words. */
export function widestRun(text: string, face: Face, size: number): number;
/** min(1, measure / widestRun(text, face, size)), clamped to floor / size (floor default 11). */
export function fitScale(text: string, face: Face, size: number, measure: number, floor?: number): number;
```

The node script writes one job file (JSON: id, master path, out paths, per-id overrides, and the constants below) and runs `python3 scripts/lib/tungsten.py --job <file> --out <tmpdir>` once for all photos (≈0.8 s per photo; ≈2.2 min for 162). Python writes lossless PNGs plus `metrics.json`; node encodes with `cwebp` exactly as it does today. Constants, one source of truth: `SETTLE` is read from `theme.ts` (`colors.liningDeep`) and `GHOST_HI` from `colors.ghostHi` by the same regex check-contrast uses, so the dissolve seam always equals the cellar ground. `POOL = '#7C5642'` lives in the script.

### 8.2 `scripts/lib/tungsten.py`: the algorithm (port of `specs/v3-mockups/cellar/grade.py`, three changes)

Work in linear light (sRGB transfer, as grade.py).

1. `sat = (max−min)/max`, `lum` (Rec. 709 on linear), `bg = median(lum of the top 1/8 rows)`.
2. Light pool: Gaussian at (cx 0.50, cy 0.46), rx 0.40, ry 0.46, `pool = exp(−(dx²+dy²)·1.1)`.
3. Backdrop mask (grade.py verbatim): `bd = unsat · notShadow · notNearHighlight`, `unsat = clip(1 − (sat−0.06)/0.14)`, `notShadow = clip((lum − 0.25bg)/(0.25bg))`, highlight threshold `bg·(1.40 + 2.5(1−pool)²)`, softness `0.35bg`; Gaussian blur 3px.
4. `wall = (SETTLE + (POOL − SETTLE)·pool) · clip(lum/bg, 0, 1.6)^1.3`; `subject = lin · (1.06, 0.98, 0.88) · (0.55 + 0.45·pool)`; `out = mix(subject, wall, bd)`.
5. **New: stray-highlight substitution.** grade.py leaves bright napkins, sugar piles and table reflections glowing away from the glass (visible in the mockup's Negroni and Aviation). Compute a mask-free wash: `wash = lin · (1.00, 0.97, 0.90) · (SETTLE + (POOL − SETTLE)·pool′)/bg` with a wider pool′ (rx 0.42, ry 0.50), blended back toward `subject` where `sat > 0.15` inside the pool. Then `core = clip((pool − 0.40)/0.30)`, `m = clip((L(out)/L(wash) − 1.5)/1.0) · (1 − core)`, `m = blur(clip(1.5m), 6px)`, `out = mix(out, wash, m)`. Measured on Negroni, Aviation, Zombie, Paper Plane, Ramos Gin Fizz and White Negroni: the patches go and pale drinks keep their whites.
6. **Changed: settle colour.** Edges settle to `SETTLE` (`liningDeep #2F070D`), not `#140E0D`: `e = clip(1 − d/0.10)²·0.85`, `out = mix(out, SETTLE, e)`.
7. Back to sRGB, 1024², PNG.

Ghost (from the lit result, not the master): sRGB luminance, stretched so its 2nd percentile → 0 and 99.5th → 1 (the per-image contrast floor, so a Gin Fizz is as legible as a Negroni), gamma 1.1, emboss `t′ = clip(0.85t + 2.2·(blur₁(t) − blur₁(t) shifted 1px down-right))`, mapped linearly from `liningDeep` to `ghostHi`, Lanczos to 256², PNG. It is drawn about 2× up in a Dex window; the softness is intended (an impression, not a photo).

Per-id overrides in the node script: `GRADE_OVERRIDES = { [id]: { cx?, cy?, mode?: 'masked' | 'wash' } }`, empty at first; `wash` uses step 5's wash alone for a photo whose mask fails.

### 8.3 QA gates

`--qa <dir>` writes `lit.png` and `ghost.png` (contact sheets, 12 per row at 160px, id under each) and `flags.json`. Gates, from `metrics.json`:

| Gate | Measure | Threshold | On failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seam | max per-channel difference from `SETTLE` over the outer 1% ring | ≤ 6/255 | **build fails** (the dissolve depends on it) |
| Stray light | share of pixels with pool < 0.25 brighter than 2× the wall there | ≤ 0.4% | flagged |
| Subject | p99 luminance inside the core | ≥ 0.20 | flagged (drink crushed) |
| Ghost | luminance std inside the core | ≥ 0.05 | flagged (impression too faint) |

The report prints lit and ghost bytes and the flag count. Measured on three: lit Negroni 29 KB, Ramos 15 KB, Zombie 44 KB; ghosts 1.2 to 3.9 KB. The two sets together come to roughly 5 MB, down from 16.5 MB (11 MB colour + 5.5 MB locked) today.

P runs the script, commits the regenerated `assets/drinks/**` and `src/data/drinkPhotos.ts`, and hands Jan the QA folder (§15).
