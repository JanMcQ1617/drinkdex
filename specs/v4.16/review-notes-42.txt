Sign-in required? Yes. Reviewers can sign up with any email; no invite code or gate.

User-generated content. Posts, photos and usernames from other users. Report and block are in the "..." menu on any post or profile not yours: report offers a reason; block is immediate and symmetric, and removes any follow between the two. Undo a block in Profile → "..." → Settings → Blocked accounts. Captions, bios, display names and usernames pass a server-side filter that refuses slurs and explicit sexual terms before saving.

Tags. A post can tag up to 10 people its author follows, never anyone they are blocked with either way. Anyone tagged can remove themselves from the post's "..." menu; a post with tags is reported like any other.

Account deletion. Profile → "..." (top right) → Settings → Delete account. Deletes the account, all posts, all photos and all follows. No email or support request needed.

Age rating. 18+. Sipply is for people of legal drinking age where they live: its first screens ask, and stop anyone who answers no, and every new account confirms it. The app is about alcoholic drinks and refers to alcohol throughout. It does not sell alcohol, does not facilitate purchase, and contains no commerce of any kind.

The Dex. A reference guide of 7,948 entries: 899 cocktails with recipes, 1,428 spirits and 5,621 wines: 2,350 regions, styles and grapes, with a Wine Atlas of named wines by country and grape, and 3,271 bottles filed under them (63 entries new in this build). An entry gives style, origin, strength, glass and how it is served; none has a price, a seller or a link to buy. People log drinks they have had; nothing counts amounts or suggests drinking more. The filters (type, flavour, strength by ABV, style, grape, country) describe what is in the glass.

Contacts. The app asks for contacts access; contacts are not collected.

Third-party accounts? Sign in with Apple and Continue with Google, beside email. Apple is shown wherever Google is (guideline 4.8); either asks a new account to pick a username first. Google asks for openid, email and profile only, in the system sign-in sheet; no Google SDK. Other outside services: Supabase (hosting, sign-in), Expo (app updates).

Daily ideas. Optional local notifications, at most one a day, at a time the person picks (6 PM default). An in-app screen with an example offers them only after a first drink, post, save or follow; iOS asks only after Allow. On or off in Settings → Notifications. Messages invite logging drinks already had, writing down recipes, reading about and saving drinks; none suggests drinking, a time to drink or drinking more. To see the offer, post a drink.

People notifications (optional). A tag, a tournament invite or a new follower ("ana started following you."). Their own in-app screen asks first, then iOS. Three switches in Settings → Notifications.

Tournaments. A shared log over 1 to 31 calendar days: most different entries wins, or First to a number the host sets. People join by invite or with a code the host shares; blocks apply. Each drink counts once; amounts are never counted. Only 3 entries a day count per person; a drink first posted after them never counts: nothing rewards posting a lot at once. No entry fees, streaks or meters. Each tournament screen shows "No prizes · Apple is not a sponsor · Drink responsibly"; its "i" key opens the full rules, ending "Apple is not a sponsor. Please drink responsibly." Names pass the caption filter. To try: trophy tab → Host a tournament or Join with code.

Places. A post can name the bar or restaurant it was had at, found with on-device Apple Maps (MapKit). Only venues, never a home or address. Location is When In Use, asked only on a tap of Current place, and never reaches our server; search by name works without it. Pages count people who had a drink at a place, never amounts, prices, deals or rankings. Wrong places are reported from the post's "..." menu. Try it on iOS 18+: post a drink, then Add place.
