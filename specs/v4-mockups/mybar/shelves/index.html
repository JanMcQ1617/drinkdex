My Bar, shelvesWhat you have is one inventory, grouped by type in a fixed order and A to Z inside each shelf. Nothing in it is ranked, so nothing moves. "You can make 31 ›" is the payoff at the top; one line under it names the next bottle. You add from a shelf's "+ Add" or from the search glyph; both open the same picker, whose order never changes. Every number is worked out from src/data/barIndex.json (tools/data.py), with sloe gin and the fruit brandies taken out of the gin and brandy families (spec.md 7.4). Daylight palette, 440 × 956.
