Fixes before building

  1. My Bar, all palettes: lighten the tile wall. Tiles 52 → 48 tall with a 16 gutter both ways, and move "Add orange → 5 more drinks" under "You can make 30" as its second line in muted, not a separate row. The screen then reads title, one row, field, tiles.
  2. Home: show five whole story rings instead of six with one cut off, and give seen stories no ring at all (just the photo), so the row is quieter than the post.
  3. Night and Split post detail: fill the heart, as Daylight does, because nico.pours is in the likers list.
  4. Night photos: drop the cut-out re-bake. It leaves halos and a ghost Paper Plane. Use the studio masters on a #0E0E0E print mat instead.
  5. Split proofs: use Daylight's baked proofs (white/img/bw) with the 7% print edge. The current ones disappear on white.
  6. Split chrome: pushed screens and the likers sheet use a page-colour bar, not black; the + field drops its outline ring.
  7. Dex: keep the Nº rail strictly scroll-only and test that searching "127" jumps and outlines the tile; that is now the only way to reach a number at rest.
