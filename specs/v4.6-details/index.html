Sipply: details and the way in

v4.6, 23 Oct 2026. Two questions: which delicate red detail system makes the app less bland, and what motion carries the intro film into the app. Four detail systems and three transitions were built, scored by three judges (taste, craft, build, out of 10 each), and the top two of each were refined once.

Jan: “a short part after the intro so instead of just disappearing … it has some sort of motion design that transitions into the app … the red accent, but delicate details, making it appear as if it was simple yet complex.” Standing rule from 9 Oct: less stuff. Every mark below is ornament, not information.

1

The details

Today, for comparison (v4.5, campari, new wordmark, ink tab line)

Finalist

Pen marks v2

judges 21 / 30 · taste 8 · craft 6 · build 7

The logo's paint pen comes back at hairline weight: a drawn rule under Home's stories, a swash under tab-root titles (Dex, Profile), and one drip off the caught Nº plate. One mark per screen.

At 3x, 1:1, before and after

ForThe only system that feels like Sipply: the hand that wrote the logo signs the page. Ownable, answers “too bland” on a second look, adds nothing to read, and its rule draws on as the intro ends.
AgainstThree marks only, so it may still feel quiet. The 1 pt waver could read as wobble on a real device; the fallback is a straight rule that only tapers.
Finalist

Bar ledger v2

judges 21.5 / 30 · taste 5.5 · craft 8 · build 8

A bartender's ruled ledger: one straight Campari ruling, 2 device px, placed where a ledger printer would put one. A double rule under Home's stories, a head rule under Dex sections, margin and column rules on the drink page and Profile, a 24 pt entry rule closing each post.

At 3x, 1:1, before and after

ForThe most disciplined and cheapest to build: one motif, crisp whole-pixel lines, plain Views, nothing animated, nothing per grid cell.
AgainstAt arm's length Dex and Profile look unchanged, and a straight red line is a mark any app could have. It answers “delicate” better than “bland”.

Also built, not taken forward

Cocktail menu 19.5 / 30

A diamond divider (the most delicate single mark of the four), dotted leaders and corner brackets. Leaders point the eye at counts, brackets read as a camera viewfinder.

Print proof 16 / 30

Crop and registration marks. Clever for designers, but they read as rendering glitches, the crosshair reads as a target, and it needs a seventh accent place.

The way in: intro to app

Each preview starts on the film's last frame (the finished wordmark on white) and ends on Home. Looping, muted, at 390 × 844 pt. transitions.mp4 plays both back to back.

Finalist

Dock v2

judges 23 / 30 · taste 8 · craft 7.5 · build 7.5

The wordmark you just watched flies up and becomes the logo in Home's bar (600 ms), while the page fills from the bottom up: tab bar and keys at 160 ms, feed at 240 ms, stories at 360 ms. Pen's drawn rule signs the page under the stories from 560 to 840 ms. Done in about 0.9 s.

ForInevitable rather than decorative: the logo becomes the logo. Reads like the best iOS launches and needs no new red.
AgainstTouches the most of Home (bar keys, feed, tab bar). Bottom-up fill may read as backwards; the pen beat is quiet on a first watch.
Finalist

Hairline curtain v2

judges 20.5 / 30 · taste 5.5 · craft 6.5 · build 8.5

A pen stroke runs out from under the drip's bead, parts into a double rule, and the white splits along it: the top half carries the logo up into the bar, the bottom half drops away, and Home develops in the gap. 820 ms in all, no red left by 400 ms.

ForThe most self-contained build: it sits above Home and touches nothing inside it, so a stall leaves Home untouched. Pairs naturally with the ledger.
AgainstThe most decorative and least inevitable; a dissolve under a wipe can read as two ideas. Only Home was rendered.

Also built, not taken forward

The drop 19 / 30

The logo's drip falls and becomes the + key. The most ownable idea, but on a phone the 9 pt drop is a speck falling one way while the wordmark flies the other, and it is the costliest and riskiest to build.

Recommendation

Details: Pen marks. It is the only system that is Sipply's own (the logo's hand at hairline weight), it answers “too bland” without adding anything to read, and one mark per screen keeps to “less stuff”.

Transition: Dock. The film's wordmark becomes the app's wordmark, so the motion feels inevitable rather than decorative, and its closing beat is Pen's rule drawing on, so the two choices read as one idea.
