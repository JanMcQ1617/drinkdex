Recommendation: build Brass, with these grafts

  1. From Glow: the Dex gauge becomes a spectrum of marks, one per caught drink at its number (in brass on the gauge well, not rainbow), so 1.8% stops looking empty.
  2. From Glow: a number rail on the Dex's right edge (0001 to 2089, ticks every 100) to jump by number. Jan asked for the Dex by number; 2,089 needs a scrubber.
  3. From Glow: small ingredient glyphs in the My Bar checklist (citrus discs in their fruit colour, bottle silhouettes), drawn as tiny SVGs. This is where real colour beyond wine and bone comes in without glows.
  4. From Glow: counts on the Dex filters (Cocktails 899, Spirits 1,190) and the likers sheet split into "People you follow 4" and "Others 9", each row with "liked 2h ago".
  5. From Menu: Home's dateline under the wordmark ("Tuesday, 7 October · 6 friends posted today") in onLiningMuted.
  6. From Menu: "One ingredient away" leads with the best single bottle to add ("+10 drinks · Add dry vermouth · opens Martini, Clarito, Bronx and 7 more") before the per-drink rows.
  7. From Menu: the "See all" list of drinks you can make set as a menu, name, dotted leader, Nº right-aligned, ingredients underneath.
  8. From Menu: numbered likers rows (01, 02, 03) and the Dex search placeholder "Search by name or Nº", plus a range head over each block of the grid ("Nº 0001 – 0009 · 4 of these 9 in your Dex").
  9. Fix in Brass before building: the mock's Profile plaque says 64 in the Dex while the Dex header says 38; the plaque must read the Dex count, not posts.
  10. Keep Brass's open fallback: if Jan wants the feed full-bleed, the brackets move to inset 12 on the full photo.
