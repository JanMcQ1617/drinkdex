Sipply v3.3, direction "Menu" Menu card and print: the app as a printed cocktail list and the bar ephemera around it

Same Cabinet grounds (bone paper, wine lining), now printed in two inks: wine for headings, ornaments and the one action, plus a bottle-green second ink for everything that is yours (Nº numerals, the "In your Dex" stamp, ticks, meters). Three paper stocks instead of one cream: the page, bone card for tickets and cards, and a warmer buff for stubs. Posts are ticket stubs with a perforated tear line, the Dex is a numbered list (one-ink proofs for drinks not yet caught), My Bar is an order pad with a ledger margin and a menu with dotted leaders, likers are a numbered guest list. No gradient anywhere at the top of a screen. 440 x 956 frames, real catalogue photos and Dex numbers, counts from barIndex.json.
